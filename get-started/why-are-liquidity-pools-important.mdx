Liquidity pools are the backbone of decentralized finance. Whether you're building a new token, a DApp, or a DeFi service, it starts with liquidity. A new token has no market until you create a liquidity pool that enables swaps between your token and others.

Deep liquidity for key tokens like SOL enables smooth liquidation and reduces bad debt risk. Deep liquidity for wrapped tokens (e.g., BTC, ETH) on Solana lets users bridge assets across chains, which brings in users from other networks.

Most users see only the DeFi app. Behind every trade is a liquidity pool.

### Ways to Provide Liquidity

You can be a liquidity provider (LP) for:

- New token launches
- Memecoins or non-hyped assets
- Market-making strategies
- Major DeFi protocols or smaller experiments
- Real-world assets

### Who Provides Liquidity

Liquidity comes from:

- Professional Market Makers
- Developers who integrate liquidity pools into DApps
- Creators who launch and bootstrap liquidity for new tokens
- Launchpads that help migrate and establish liquidity for new projects
- Everyday DeFi users who provide liquidity directly to earn yield or support projects they believe in

### Liquidity and the Future of Crypto

Meteora expects millions of people to launch billions of new tokens on decentralized systems rather than centralized exchanges. Creating, launching, distributing, and maintaining those tokens all depend on liquidity pools.

LPs create, fund, and maintain the markets that make DeFi possible.

Launchpads give new tokens a place to be created and launched. The launchpad ecosystem is still early.
