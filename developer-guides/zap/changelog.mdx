  ## Program: `zap` `0.3.0`

  Program ID (mainnet and devnet): `zapvX9M3uf5pvy4wRPAbQgdQsM1xmuiFnkfHKPvwMiz`

  ### Added

  - Added endpoint `zap_in_dlmm_pro`. It opens a DLMM Pro position for the given tick ranges and adds `multiplier * weight` liquidity to every range in one deposit, sized by the largest multiplier both ledger balances can fund. The binding side is used up.
  - The other side's leftover goes into its single-sided range on the optional `leftover` (`range_x` above the price, `range_y` below it), and only that range is opened, after the shape ranges. With no `leftover`, that balance stays in the ledger.
  - A leftover range is given by its bound next to the price (`inner_tick_id`) and away from it (`outer_tick_id`). With `leftover.is_inner_tick_relative`, `inner_tick_id` is a distance in tick spacing away from the price, from the closest tick a single-sided range can start at for the pool tick at execution, so the leftover ranges start next to the price. With `leftover`, the tick arrays can be passed in any order, and extra accounts are allowed.
  - `max_tick_slippage` is the maximum distance between the pool tick at execution and `current_tick_id`. The optional `min_multiplier` fails the instruction with `ExceededSlippage` when the multiplier at execution is below it. A move inside a single tick can change the multiplier many times over when a range bound sits close to the price.
  - Range bounds and the leftover `outer_tick_id` must be within DLMM Pro's min and max tick (`[-443636, 443636]`).
  - `zap_out` supports swapping through DLMM Pro (`dPro1D5wiqnGMH8vMDG23jh35btTHoWdyqXjqkqxe8n`) `swap`.

  ### Changed

  - Updated Anchor to `1.2.1` (`solana-v4` crates), Solana to `4.1.2`, and Rust to `1.95.0`. The program is built with platform-tools `v1.57` for SBPF `v3`.
  - Bumped the `damm-v2` (cp-amm) dependency to the Anchor `1.2.1` revision.

  ## TypeScript SDK: `@meteora-ag/zap-sdk` `2.1.0`

  [SDK PR #41](https://github.com/MeteoraAg/zap-sdk/pull/41)

  <Tip>
    Production: [`@meteora-ag/zap-sdk@2.1.0`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@meteora-ag/zap-sdk/v/2.1.0).
  </Tip>

  ### Added

  - Support for Zap program `0.3.0`: endpoint `zap_in_dlmm_pro`, and `zap_out` swaps through DLMM Pro.
  - `zapOutThroughDlmmPro` zaps out by swapping through a DLMM Pro pool.
  - `getZapInDlmmProDirectParams`, `getZapInDlmmProIndirectParams`, and `buildZapInDlmmProTransaction` zap in to a new DLMM Pro position. The position is given as tick ranges with weights, for example the `ranges` from `buildLiquidityShape` of `@meteora-ag/dlmm-pro-sdk`. `maxTickSlippage` and the optional `minMultiplier` bound the price slippage, and the optional `leftover` ranges take what the ranges leave of the token that is not used up. The position NFT mint passed to `buildZapInDlmmProTransaction` must sign the zap-in transaction.
  - `estimateDlmmProDirectSwap` and `estimateDlmmProIndirectSwap` size the swaps of a DLMM Pro zap in. They also return the pool tick and the multiplier expected when the zap in executes, to pass as the tick the ranges were built against and to derive `minMultiplier` from.
  - `getDlmmProZapInMultiplier`, which mirrors how the program sizes the deposit, and `getDlmmProZapInTickArrays`, which returns the tick arrays the zap in needs, including the ones the `leftover` ranges can resolve to.
  - Helpers `getDlmmProSwapInstruction`, `getDlmmProTickArraysForSwap`, `getDlmmProDepositTransferHookAccounts`, and `deriveDlmmProEventAuthority`.
  - Constants `DLMM_PRO_PROGRAM_ID` and `AMOUNT_IN_DLMM_PRO_OFFSET`.
  - Examples `zapInDlmmProDirect`, `zapInDlmmProIndirect`, and `zapOutThroughDlmmPro`.

  ### Changed

  - Added the dependency `@meteora-ag/dlmm-pro-sdk`.
  - The IDL of release `0.3.0` names the types it shares between the pool programs after their program. In `ZapTypes`, `remainingAccountsInfo`, `remainingAccountsSlice`, and `accountsType` are now `dlmmDlmmTypesRemainingAccountsInfo`, `dlmmDlmmTypesRemainingAccountsSlice`, and `dlmmDlmmTypesAccountsType`, and `pool` is now `cpAmmStatePoolPool`. The methods of the SDK take the same arguments as before.
</Update>

  ## Release summary

  | Component | Version | PR |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | Program | `0.2.3` | — |

  ## What We Shipped
  - Whitelisted Jupiter `route_v2` and `shared_account_route_v2` for `zap_out`
  - `zap_out` now derives the `amount_in` offset from the AMM program and its instruction discriminator
  - Updated Anchor to `1.0.2`
  - Deprecated Jupiter `route` and `shared_account_route` in favor of the v2 routes. Both stay whitelisted for `zap_out`
  - `offset_amount_in` in `ZapOutParameters` is ignored. The field stays in the instruction layout

  ## Program: `zap` `0.2.3`

  Program ID (mainnet and devnet): `zapvX9M3uf5pvy4wRPAbQgdQsM1xmuiFnkfHKPvwMiz`

  ### Changed

  - Update anchor to `1.0.2`
  - Endpoint `zap_out` now derives the offset of `amount_in` in the swap payload from the amm program and its instruction discriminator, instead of reading it from `ZapOutParameters`.

  ### Added

  - Add Jupiter's `route_v2` and `shared_account_route_v2` as whitelisted instructions for `zap_out`

  ### Deprecated

  - Jupiter's `route` and `shared_account_route` are deprecated in favor of `route_v2` and `shared_account_route_v2`. They remain whitelisted for `zap_out` for backward compatibility.
  - `offset_amount_in` in `ZapOutParameters` is ignored. It remains in the instruction layout for backward compatibility.
</Update>
