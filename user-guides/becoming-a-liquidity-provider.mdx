## What is a Liquidity Provider?

As a liquidity provider (LP), you deposit tokens into a liquidity pool, and traders swap against them.

## Why Become a Liquidity Provider?

You earn fees or rewards whenever a trade uses the liquidity you deposited, typically in proportion to your share of the pool:
- Swap fees (also known as LP fees)
- Bonus incentives (e.g. yield farming rewards, protocol incentives)

<Note>You may not get the same amount of tokens back as you deposited. Because traders swap against your tokens in exchange for an LP fee, price changes on one or both tokens can leave you with less of one token and more of the other.</Note>

## What is an AMM?

An Automated Market Maker (AMM) is a type of decentralized exchange (DEX) protocol.

Traditional exchanges match individual buyers and sellers through a centralized orderbook, usually with an intermediary. AMMs use smart contracts on Solana, and traders trade against the tokens deposited in a liquidity pool.

An AMM sets prices algorithmically with a pricing formula. The most common is the “constant product” formula, `x * y = k`, where:
- `x` = amount of Token A in the pool
- `y` = amount of Token B in the pool
- `k` = a constant value that never changes

When someone trades, the AMM adjusts the token balances so the product `x * y` stays constant. The more users buy one token, the more expensive it becomes in the pool. The more users sell it, the cheaper it becomes.

- For **liquidity providers (LPs)**: LPs deposit token pairs into liquidity pools so traders can trade against those tokens. In a constant product AMM, the two tokens are usually deposited at equal USD value (50:50). For example, LPs can deposit an equal value of SOL and USDC into a SOL-USDC pool.

- For **traders**: A trader or another smart contract interacts directly with the pool's smart contract to swap one token for the other, for example SOL for USDC in a SOL-USDC pool. The exchange rate comes from the pool's formula and the tokens available in the pool, so trades are non-custodial and need no intermediary.

**LP Fees**: When trades use the tokens in a liquidity pool, that pool's LPs earn a portion of the fees based on their share of the total liquidity.

Meteora's DAMM v1 and DAMM v2 are examples of such AMMs.

## Differences Between DLMM and DAMM v1/v2

### DLMM

Meteora’s DLMM (Dynamic Liquidity Market Maker) pools concentrate liquidity in 0-slippage bins, with flexible volatility strategies and dynamic fees.

The liquidity of an asset pair is organized into discrete price bins. Tokens in a bin swap at that bin's price, so swaps within a bin have 0 slippage and 0 price impact. The market for the pair is the aggregate of all its bins.

LPs select a volatility strategy and make the price range narrower or wider to concentrate liquidity. The higher capital efficiency lets a DLMM position support more volume, and earn more fees, than the same liquidity on a typical DEX.

DLMM also allows single-sided deposits, so LPs can deposit one token to DCA (dollar cost average) into the other token in the pair. Single-sided deposits also suit token launches: the project deposits only its base token first, and users buy it with USDC or SOL when the pool starts trading.

LPs earn dynamic fees, which rise with market volatility.

A DLMM pool can become "inactive" and stop earning fees whenever the active price moves out of the range set by the LP. DLMM pools therefore need more active management than Dynamic AMM pools.

DLMM pools also don't provide LP tokens upon adding liquidity, so once the pool is created, liquidity deposited cannot be locked permanently (unlike dynamic AMM pools).

Read an overview of DLMM [here](https://meteoraag.medium.com/dlmm-new-dynamic-liquidity-protocol-to-boost-lp-fees-on-solana-84867bad0907). Any user can create a new DLMM pool [here](https://app.meteora.ag/create). 

### DAMM v1

DAMM v1 (Dynamic AMM v1) pools use a constant product AMM model and are simpler for LPs to use.

Unlike DLMM, DAMM v1 pools have a fixed fee %, do not let LPs concentrate liquidity, and operate across the full price range, so they never become inactive. LPs do not need to manage or rebalance them.

Assets in dynamic pools are deposited into the vaults in the yield layer, so SOL/USDC/USDT assets are dynamically allocated to external lending protocols to generate yield and rewards for LPs. LPs earn from the AMM trading fees, the SOL/USDC/USDT lending interest, and any liquidity mining rewards collected from the platforms.

Creating a dynamic pool is permissionless: any user or developer can create one without approval from the Meteora team.

In addition, with DAMM v1 pools, [memecoin creators have the option to "burn" their liquidity](https://x.com/MeteoraAG/status/1786045723953557507) by permanently locking the Meteora LP tokens (which represent the liquidity in a dynamic pool). Memecoin creators can compound and claim fees on permanently locked liquidity forever, even though they no longer have access to that liquidity. Memecoin creators can consider launching their memecoin with a Memecoin Pool, which is a subset of dynamic pools and has features specially catered to memecoin launches (e.g. a dynamic fee % schedule).

Read an overview of DAMM v1 [here](/legacy-products/damm-v1/what-is-damm-v1). Any user can create a new DAMM v1 pool [here](https://app.meteora.ag/create).

### DAMM v2

DAMM v2 (Dynamic AMM v2) is also a constant-product AMM that needs little upkeep from LPs. It adds configurability and features over DAMM v1 for LPs, token launches, and launchpads.

Key features include:
- SPL and Token 2022 support
- Dynamic Fee, which raises fees during high volatility
- Anti-Sniper mechanisms such as the Fee Scheduler (fees start higher at launch and drop over time). Rate Limiter still runs on existing pools and is not available for a new pool.
- Fee collection in Base + Quote, Quote only, or Quote + Compounding. In compounding mode, a configured share of the LP fee is added back to reserves. `0` leaves that fee claimable in the quote token.
- Transferable liquidity position NFT, so you can give ownership of your position to someone else
- Farming mechanism that is built directly into the program, not as a separate farm program
- Greater cost efficiency; creating a single DAMM v2 pool with a liquidity position costs ~0.022 SOL, compared to ~0.25 SOL for a DLMM Launch Pool
- Different options to lock liquidity; option to lock liquidity with vesting (non-permanent) or permanently, while still allowing fee claims.
- Single-sided liquidity pools using only one token for greater launch flexibility (e.g. launch your token without requiring USDC)
- Concentrated liquidity; at pool creation, developers can configure a preferred min-max price range for the pool to enable higher capital efficiency for the deposited liquidity.

Read an overview of DAMM v2 [here](https://meteoraag.medium.com/dynamic-amm-v2-helping-lps-and-launches-win-c56128c883ad). Any user can create a new DAMM v2 pool [here](https://app.meteora.ag/create).
